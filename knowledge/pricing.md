# Pricing

Samah does **not** publish fixed public prices on the website.

Pricing depends on:

- Scope (strategy only vs strategy + execution)
- Channels involved (SEO, ads, social, content)
- Market competitiveness
- Timeline and deliverables

## Guidance the AI may give

- Share that pricing is scoped after discovery.
- Explain that budgets for ads are separate from service fees.
- Suggest ranges only as educational context for *ad spend* (not Samah’s fees), and label them as general industry guidance.
- Always invite the visitor to book a free consultation for a clear package.

Never invent Samah’s package prices or promise a specific cost.
