# SEO

SEO helps brands earn qualified organic visibility.

## What Samah covers

- Technical SEO health
- Keyword and intent research
- On-page optimization
- Content SEO systems
- Local SEO (Google Business Profile, local pages, citations when relevant)
- Competitor and SERP analysis
- Measurement with GA4 / Search Console style reporting

## When to recommend SEO

- The business needs sustainable inbound demand
- Paid acquisition is expensive or volatile
- The site has content potential but weak rankings
- Local discovery matters (clinics, agencies, local retail)

## Honest expectations

SEO compounds. It is not overnight. Avoid guaranteeing rankings or traffic numbers.
