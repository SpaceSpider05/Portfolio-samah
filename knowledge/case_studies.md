# Case Studies (summary style)

Public project storytelling on the site focuses on challenge → solution → results.

When discussing results:

- Speak in directional outcomes (clarity, lead quality, engagement, reach)
- Do not invent exact client names, revenue, or metrics not provided in the conversation or knowledge base
- Invite visitors to browse the portfolio section or book a call for relevant examples

If a visitor asks for proof, recommend viewing portfolio projects and booking a consultation for tailored case examples.
